# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - The Open Group Website

## 1. Project Overview
The Open Group is a multi-brand corporate entity. This project involves building a new group website that showcases all its brands, communicates the group's mission, highlights its commitment to sustainability, provides career opportunities, and offers contact information.

## 2. Target Audience
- Potential business partners and clients for individual brands.
- Prospective employees looking for careers within the group.
- Investors and stakeholders interested in the group's performance and sustainability efforts.
- General public seeking information about the group's mission and values.

## 3. Core Brands
The website will feature the following 14 brands, each with its own tagline:
1. **OpenSpace**: Creating Spaces. Unlocking Potential.
2. **OpenComms**: Connecting Businesses Without Limits.
3. **OpenWaste**: Smarter Waste Solutions For a Clearer Tomorrow.
4. **OpenEnergy**: Powering a Sustainable Future Together.
5. **OpenWorks**: Facilitating Better Facilities.
6. **OpenFleet**: Keeping Business Moving Forward.
7. **OpenAqua**: Pure Connections. Clearer Business.
8. **OpenTalent**: Recruiting. A Better Way.
9. **OpenSure**: Safer, Compliant and Always Prepared.
10. **OpenTech**: Technology Solutions That Drive Results.
11. **OpenCapital**: Funding Growth, Fuelling Ambition.
12. **OpenCover**: Protecting Businesses. Supporting Success.
13. **Openlead**: The Smarter Way To Source New Business.
14. **OpenAcademy**: Developing People. Building Brighter Futures.

**Group Slogan**: CONNECTING BUSINESSES. SUPPORTING SUCCESS.

## 4. Key Sections & Functional Requirements
### 4.1 Home Page
- **Hero Section**: Strong visual presence with the group slogan and mission.
- **Brand Showcase**: A grid or interactive section displaying all 14 brands with their logos and taglines.
- **Mission Statement**: High-level overview of the group's purpose.

### 4.2 Sustainability
- Details of the group's commitment to sustainable practices.
- specific initiatives or goals (placeholders to be refined).

### 4.3 Group Mission & Values
- Detailed mission statement: "CONNECTING BUSINESSES. SUPPORTING SUCCESS."
- Core values that drive the group.

### 4.4 Careers
- Information about working at The Open Group.
- A section for current openings (can be a list or a link to a portal).
- Culture and benefits overview.

### 4.5 Contact Details
- Contact form.
- Physical address, email, and phone number.
- Links to social media profiles.

## 5. Design & Aesthetics
- **Style**: Modern, Professional, Clean, and High-end.
- **Color Palette**: Primary colors based on the logo (Black and Cyan/Aqua). Use of negative space for a premium feel.
- **Typography**: Refined, sans-serif fonts for a corporate yet modern look (e.g., Montserrat, Lato, or a custom geometric sans-serif).
- **Interactions**: Smooth transitions, subtle hover effects on brand cards, and staggered animations for content reveal.

## 6. Technical Requirements
- Responsive design (Desktop-first as per guidelines).
- SEO optimized.
- Fast loading times with optimized assets.
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1).
